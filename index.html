A Warm Sanctuary Crafted for Coffee Enthusiasts

Founded in 2021, Chuska Cafe was born out of a simple yearning: to create an unhurried, lush sanctuary where the aroma of freshly ground single-origin beans meets honest, handcrafted cuisine.

We partner directly with sustainable coffee estates across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Chikmagalur. From our slow cold brews to our flaky double-baked almond croissants, nothing is rushed. We invite you to stay a while, read a book under the hanging vines, or share laughs over steaming mugs.
